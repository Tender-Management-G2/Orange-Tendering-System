## Explicitly reject
- Thousands of suppliers, global market maps, country data, ownership, supply chains, spend, ESG, arbitrary risk/confidence scores, or percentages not in authorized records.
- Artificial links between category groups, hub nodes pretending to be suppliers, or a graph that starts focused on one supplier.
- Neon everywhere, oversized glowing dots, decorative particles that look like data, illegible labels, equal-sized card grids, stacked scrollbars, fake latency/analysis counters.
- Copying the full-page references at their compressed image scale. Build spacious sections at actual laptop dimensions. The actual demo has about 12 suppliers and the normal team database was shown with about 20, so label the real suppliers and preserve isolated nodes.
