# Readability, copy, and honest empty states

Copy: concise title + 1-line insight + evidence link + expandable method. Never put mini-essays inside every card. Supplier names should not be forced to overlap nodes; labels anchored outside with collision avoidance. Use full text on selection and search; truncate only secondary labels with tooltip.

- No data: "No authorized records match the current filters."
- No awards: "No official awards were recorded in this period. Recommendations are not awards."
- Too small: "Insufficient comparable history to detect a repeat pattern."
- Detector not run: "Semantic comparison was not run."
- Missing timestamp: "Winner notification sent time is not recorded — lifecycle duration unavailable."
- Tender sealed: "Financial bid amounts remain sealed for this round."
- No graph links: "This supplier is registered but has no supported relationship in the selected scope."
- AI: "I could not verify this figure from records available to you."
- Upload: "This PDF contains scanned pages; text extraction may be incomplete."

Bilingual content: support AR/EN, correct directionality, numerals/units, Arabic font fallback. Every visual must have a screen-reader summary or table fallback. Motion never replaces text or evidence.
