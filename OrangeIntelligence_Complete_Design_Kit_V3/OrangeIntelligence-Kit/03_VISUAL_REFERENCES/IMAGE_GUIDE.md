## Aesthetic constraints
- Dark editorial premium, navy #0B1320, accent Orange #FF7900, carefully bounded complementary hues.
- Elegance without overpowering neon bloom. Protect chart/table readability above all.
- The Universe is a 3D ORBITAL GRAPH, not geographical earth with fictional locations.
- Camera has slow auto-orbit, physics-driven but bounded motion and pause on selection; there is a 2D/table fallback.
- Voice has meaningful real audio-reactive motion, idles gently rather than pretending to hear something.
- Typography: full names readable at real desktop resolution; no dense matrix of tiny labels.
- Images are desktop art direction; on mobile replace 3D scene with a usable focused/table fallback when needed.
