# One professional quality bar, both themes

Readable, role-safe information design before visual spectacle. Every graphic needs title/meaning/source; every suspicious line needs explanation and evidence. One filter selection must update numerical, table and visual states atomically. A page with three-dimensional spectacle but unreadable labels is a failure.

**Dark theme**: midnight navy, orange highlights and deliberate secondary categories; subtle bloom confined to active/selected nodes. **Light theme**: near-white surfaces, dark readable body, category colors adjusted for contrast, restrained shadows. No direct negative filter of dark. Avoid default ECharts fallback font 10px and off-canvas tooltips.

Responsive: desktop 1440/1920 with large main scene; laptop 1024 with collapsible inspector; mobile ~390 with 2D supplier list/search + optional lightweight orbit; no sideways overflow; allow accessible table alternative and pause. Respect zoom 200%. Avoid sticky voice overlay obscuring important controls. Prefer one scroll container per active tab; floating panel drawers must be keyboard-dismissable.
